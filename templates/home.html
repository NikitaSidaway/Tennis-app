Welcome to Cashmere Tennis Club

Who Are We?

Cashmere Tennis Club is a vibrant, community-focused hub that brings life long fun and fitness through Tennis. Our club offers outdoor courts and a large club house surrounded by the stunning backdrop of the Port Hills. We strive our solve in providing a safe environment for all ages and skill levels. From junior coaching programmes to social tennis and even competitive interclub matches, there's something for everyone.
